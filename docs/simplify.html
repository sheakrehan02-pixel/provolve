How every issue plays

The same four moves, in this order, every week.

1 · Guess

Gut check

Answer a few questions before you know anything. Your guesses stay locked until the end.

"Can an arrow turn 0.37 times around a loop?"
2 · Play

Instruments

Sliders and canvases running the real equations. You build intuition by poking the physics until it pushes back.

Squeeze Earth until light can't escape.
3 · Derive

The math

Only now do the equations arrive, step by step, each one tied to something you already played with.

ρ + pᵣ = (b′ − 1) / 8πr₀² < 0
4 · Solve

Boss level

A real problem from the field. Then a debrief puts your first guesses next to the physics.

Mystery material: what is its Chern number?
Understanding you reach through your own intuition sticks. Facts you're handed don't.

Most explainers give you a surface-level picture and a metaphor. Simplify It goes further down, to the fundamental principle the whole topic rests on, and then makes you rediscover it.

The abstract parts, the ones you can't hold in your hand or easily picture, get an instrument you can poke. Every number on these pages comes from an actual calculation, not an animation of one.
